# My Voice: phrases to record (R sounds)

**How:** iPhone Voice Memos app. One memo, quiet room, phone about a foot away.
Read each phrase once, in order, with a **2-second pause** between phrases. Don't say the numbers.
If he trips on one, stop the memo, then start a new memo from that phrase.
When done, share the memo(s) into the chat.
